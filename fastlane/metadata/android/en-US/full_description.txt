GeoCam is an ad-free GPS map camera. It stamps your location, the place
name, the date and time, a compass heading and a small mini-map directly
onto each photo, so you always know where a shot was taken.

This is the FLOSS build. It contains no proprietary libraries at all:
location comes from Android's own built-in provider and the mini-map is
drawn from OpenStreetMap, so it will install and run on devices without
Google apps.

Features:

* Mini-map stamped onto every photo, in three sizes
* Place name, coordinates, date, time, accuracy, altitude and heading
* Location stamping can be turned off entirely, in which case no location
  data is requested, drawn, or written into the image
* Coordinates shown as decimal or DMS
* Optional custom note, such as a survey point reference
* Adjustable JPEG quality and maximum image size
* Front (selfie) camera support, with flash and exposure controls
* Built-in gallery for reviewing and deleting shots
* One-tap force refresh of the current location
* Works out of the box in portrait, and rotates only when the phone is
  actually turned a quarter turn

Privacy:

No accounts. No ads. No analytics and no crash reporting. Photos are
saved straight to your device's gallery. Location is only requested if
you turn GPS stamping on, and you can also save a second clean copy with
no location data written to it at all.

A second build called "GeoCam" also exists, which uses Google Play
Services for positioning and Google map tiles for the satellite view.
That one is distributed separately and is not this listing.
